# Engagement Methodology, Edition 3

## Scoping
Every engagement opens with a two-week discovery. Discovery ends with a written scope memo signed by the client sponsor.

## Fieldwork
Fieldwork runs in two-week cycles. Each cycle ends with a written checkpoint listing findings, open questions, and any change in scope.

## Quality review
Two reviewers sign every deliverable: the engagement lead and one reviewer from outside the engagement. The outside reviewer checks evidence, not prose.

## Escalation
Any finding that would change the client's stated position is escalated to the sponsor within one business day.
