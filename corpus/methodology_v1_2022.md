# Engagement Methodology, Edition 1

## Scoping
Every engagement opens with a four-week discovery. Discovery ends with a scope memo.

## Fieldwork
Fieldwork runs in four-week cycles. Each cycle ends with a checkpoint.

## Quality review
One reviewer signs every deliverable: the engagement lead.

## Escalation
Findings are escalated at the end of the cycle.

## Appendix A: frequently asked questions
Q. What if the sponsor is unavailable during discovery?
A. Discovery may proceed and the memo is signed retroactively.

Q. What if fieldwork uncovers a scope change?
A. The change is recorded in the checkpoint and priced at the end of the engagement.

Q. Who owns the working papers?
A. The firm owns working papers. The client owns the deliverable.

Q. How long are working papers retained?
A. Working papers are retained for seven years.

## Appendix B: amendments
Amendment 2022-07: checkpoints may be delivered verbally where the client prefers.
Amendment 2023-02: the four-week cycle may be shortened to three weeks by agreement.
Amendment 2023-09: retention extended to ten years for regulated clients.
