# Engagement Review

## How we scope an engagement
Every engagement opens with a two-week discovery. `[METH-V3 §Scoping]`
Discovery ends with a written scope memo signed by the client sponsor. `[METH-V3 §Scoping]`

## How fieldwork runs
Fieldwork runs in two-week cycles. `[METH-V3 §Fieldwork]`
Each cycle ends with a written checkpoint listing findings, open questions, and any change in scope. `[METH-V3 §Fieldwork]`

## Quality review
Two reviewers sign every deliverable: the engagement lead and one reviewer from outside the engagement. `[METH-V3 §Quality review]`
The outside reviewer checks evidence, not prose. `[METH-V3 §Quality review]`

## Escalation
Any finding that would change the client's stated position is escalated to the sponsor within one business day. `[METH-V3 §Escalation]`
